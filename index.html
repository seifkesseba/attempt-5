General Information & Mission

Understanding PTN

Discover how the Peer Tutoring Network builds an empowering, reciprocal learning ecosystem inside our school community.

What is the Peer Tutoring Network (PTN)?

The Peer Tutoring Network (PTN) is an officially structured, school-collaborative academic initiative designed to connect high-achieving older students with peers seeking assistance in specific subjects.

Unlike traditional paid tutoring services, PTN leverages our school’s unique ecosystem. Peer tutors have taken the exact same courses, studied under the same teachers, and mastered the specific curricula—offering relatable, low-stress, and highly effective academic guidance.

Benefits of Being a Tutor

Why upperclassmen love tutoring with PTN

  • Resume & College Application Booster: Builds tangible proof of leadership, mentorship, and community service backed by official school records.
  • Verified Volunteer Hours: Earn official community service hours required for honors societies, NHS, or graduation credits.
  • "Learning by Teaching": Deepen your own mastery of core concepts in AP/IB subjects or advanced electives before college entrance exams.
  • Essential Soft Skills: Develop communication, empathy, public speaking, and active listening skills in a supportive environment.

Benefits of Getting Tutored

Why students thrive with peer support

  • Relatable & Comfortable Learning: Learn without the intimidation or pressure often felt around teachers or adult tutors.
  • Course-Specific Tips & Shortcuts: Get insights on specific teacher test styles, exam prep strategies, and effective study habits.
  • Flexibility & Custom Times: Request sessions at any custom time of day—whether online via Google Meet or in-person at the library.
  • Targeted Across 15+ Subjects: From Math and Physics to MUN, Arabic Identity, and Art, receive specialized help where you need it most.
Founder's Vision & Note

A Brief Note From Seif Kesseba

"I started the Peer Tutoring Network to solve a common challenge in our school: students who need quick, understandable academic help often don't know where to turn, while high-achieving upperclassmen want meaningful ways to share their knowledge and build their college applications.

PTN bridges this exact gap. By establishing mandatory coordinator interviews and verified hour logs, we ensure high quality for tutees and real recognition for tutors. Together, we're building a stronger, more collaborative school culture."

Seif Kesseba
Founder & Program Lead, Peer Tutoring Network
